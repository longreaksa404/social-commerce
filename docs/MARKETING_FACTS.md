**Status words**

- **Live**: in the code, deployed, and switched on for every seller on
  order.oaksolve.com.
- **In progress**: built or being set up, but not usable by every seller
  yet.
- **Not yet**: not built. A plan or an idea at most.

**Recording tips**

- The app opens in **Khmer** on a new phone. Button names below are given
  in English and in the app's own Khmer, so what's said matches what's on
  screen. English: Settings → Language (ភាសា).
- On a phone the seller's app has five tabs at the bottom: Orders ·
  Customers · Products · Links · Settings (កុម្ម៉ង់ · អតិថិជន · ទំនិញ · តំណ ·
  ការកំណត់).
- It's a website in the phone's browser. There is nothing to install.
- Light and dark mode both work (Settings → Theme, រូបរាង).

## 1. One-liner

**Oak Order gives your Facebook, TikTok and Instagram shop one link.
Customers order and pay through it, and you see every order, payment and
delivery in one place on your phone.**

The app's own lines, from the start page (use them word for word):

| English | Khmer |
|---|---|
| Turn social media chats into real orders | ប្រែការឆាតលើបណ្ដាញសង្គម ទៅជាការកុម្ម៉ង់ពិតៗ |
| Easy orders for sellers on Facebook, TikTok and Instagram. | ការកុម្ម៉ង់ងាយស្រួល សម្រាប់អ្នកលក់លើ Facebook, TikTok និង Instagram។ |

## 2. Who it's for

- **Small sellers in Cambodia who sell through Facebook, TikTok, Instagram
  or Telegram** and take orders in chat today: clothes and fashion, beauty
  and cosmetics, accessories, phone and electronic accessories, home
  products (01 §3.2).
- Sellers who write orders on paper, in Excel or in chat history, count
  stock by hand, and send parcels with their own driver or a courier
  (J&T Express, VET Express…).
- Sellers whose customers pay by KHQR or cash on delivery.
- One person or a small team: the owner plus up to 10 helpers.
- Their customers need nothing new: no app, no account, no password. Most
  have Telegram (the founder's estimate), which makes checkout faster
  (section 4).

These are the founder's working assumptions (01 §3.3). **No real seller
has gone live yet** (04_STATUS.md, Phase 9), so there are no customer
numbers, reviews or quotes to use.

## 4. Customer side: link → order → pay → track

1. **The link.** The customer taps the shop's link in a post, a comment
   reply or a chat. In Facebook and Telegram it shows as a picture card
   (the cover or the product photo, the name, "· Oak Order"). Live.
2. **The shop.** Cover, logo and name at the top; All and the categories;
   a grid of photos; "Only 3 left" (នៅសល់តែ 3 ទៀត) and "Sold out" (អស់ស្តុក);
   sale prices with the old price crossed out; a **Delivery and payment**
   (ការដឹកជញ្ជូន និងការបង់ប្រាក់) box. A + on a photo adds a product without
   options to the cart.
   "Made with Oak Order" (ដំណើរការដោយ Oak Order) is at the bottom. Live.
3. **A product.** Photos, options (size, colour), stock, **Add to cart**
   (ដាក់កន្ត្រក) or **Buy now** (ទិញឥឡូវនេះ), **Ask seller on Telegram**
   (សួរអ្នកលក់តាម Telegram). Live.
4. **Checkout** (cart and checkout on one page): **Continue with Telegram**
   (បន្តជាមួយ Telegram) fills in the name and a confirmed phone number, or
   **or type your details** (ឬវាយបញ្ចូលព័ត៌មានរបស់អ្នក). The phone is read
   back ("The seller will call 012 345 678. Check it's right."). Then
   **Delivery** (ការដឹកជញ្ជូន) or **Pickup** (មកយកផ្ទាល់), a courier or
   **Shop chooses**, the address and/or **Pin my location on the map**, a
   note, **KHQR** or **Cash**, and **Place order** (បញ្ជាក់ការកុម្ម៉ង់). The
   cart shows "Add $3.00 more for free delivery" when the shop has it.
   No account or password. Live.
5. **Pay.** "Thank you! Your order is placed." (អរគុណ! យើងបានទទួលការកុម្ម៉ង់
   របស់អ្នកហើយ។). KHQR: "One step left: pay $12.00 by KHQR", the QR for the
   exact amount, **Save QR to photos**, scan it in the bank app, then
   **I've paid: send receipt**. Cash: "Pay $12.00 in cash when it arrives".
   The customer sees how to pay right away, before the seller accepts.
   Live.
6. **Track.** The order page shows Ordered → Confirmed → On the way →
   Delivered (បានកុម្ម៉ង់ → បានបញ្ជាក់ → កំពុងដឹកមក → បានដល់ដៃ); for pickup,
   Ready to collect → Collected. It updates by itself. A small truck in the
   shop's header opens the order; **Your orders** (ការកុម្ម៉ង់របស់អ្នក) lists
   the orders placed on that phone. On another phone: the order link plus
   the phone number. Live.
7. **Telegram, for customers who connect it** (**Get updates on Telegram**,
   ទទួលដំណឹងតាម Telegram, on the order page, or Continue with Telegram at
   checkout), all from Oak Order's bot, all Live:
   - a receipt after ordering, with a Pay button for KHQR;
   - a message when the order is confirmed, sent and delivered;
   - one payment reminder if a KHQR order is still unpaid after 2 hours;
   - messages from the seller, with a "Reply to <shop>" button;
   - the shop opens inside Telegram, and a "🛍 <shop>" button sits beside
     the message box;
   - **OPEN** on Oak Order's bot shows **Your shops** (ហាងរបស់អ្នក): the

## 5. Pricing, trial and setup (as built)

From 01 §33.1 and §33.2, checked in `backend/app/services/plan.py`,
Settings → Plan and the Terms page (`/terms`, Fees).

- **One plan: Oak Order Standard, $9.99 a month or $99 a year**, per shop,
  in US dollars. Yearly shows "Save $20.88" against 12 monthly payments.
  It includes every feature, up to 10 staff logins, and hosting and
  updates ("What's included", អ្វីដែលមានក្នុងគម្រោង).
- **A 30-day free trial that starts when the seller opens the shop**, not
  at sign-up. Until then the shop is in setup with no time limit: the
  seller adds products and settings and can look at the shop; customers
  see "Opening soon" (នឹងបើកឆាប់ៗនេះ) and can't order. Open shop asks
  first: "Customers can order from today. Free until <date>, then $9.99 a
  month or $99 a year. **No card, no automatic charges.**"
- **No free plan, no early-seller discount, no card.** "Made with Oak
  Order" shows on every shop and can't be removed.
- **When the trial or a paid month ends:** a reminder on the Orders tab in
  the last 6 days; then 7 more days to pay while the shop keeps taking
  orders; from the 8th day, no new orders until the seller pays. Customers
  then see "This shop isn't taking orders right now", never anything about
  payment. The seller can still finish open orders and export them.
  Paying reopens the shop at once. Nothing is deleted. Paying early loses
  no days.
- **How sellers pay Oak Order: by hand.** Settings → Plan → the Pay button
  ("Pay early: $9.99" during the trial) shows the shop's code (e.g.
  OAK-K7Q2MX) to put in the transfer note, and Oak Order's KHQR for the
  exact price (set up on the live site, founder 2026-10-27), with **Save
  QR to photos** (រក្សាទុក QR ក្នុងរូបថត). The seller pays from their
  bank app and taps **I've paid** (ខ្ញុំបានបង់). Oak Order checks it by hand,
  "usually within a day". Telegram reminders go on the first day after the
  paid time ends and on day 5.
- **Setup help (01 §33.2):** creating the shop and guiding the seller
  through setup are always included. **Adding the seller's products and
  training are free for the first 3–5 sellers**, done by the founder by
  hand. The app doesn't show or count this offer, and 01 gives a range,
  not a number: say "for our first sellers", not a count, unless the
  founder fixes one. Prices after that aren't set; the starting ideas in
  01 aren't to be published.
- **Free time (internal, not an offer):** the founder can give any shop
  free time from the admin page with a $0 payment (Oak Order admin → a
  shop → **Mark paid**, amount 0). Checked 2026-10-27: a shop whose orders
  had stopped took orders again, its plan read active for a month, and the
  payment shows as $0.00 paid.
